Bangladesh’s Experience with the Adoption of Interest Rate Targeting

Why did Bangladesh’s overnight rate move away from its target after the 2023 switch to interest rate targeting? A reserve demand model and daily evidence across three operating regimes are consistent with the central bank’s limited ability to supply reserves.
